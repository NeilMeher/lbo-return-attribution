## Reconcile downside debt service in year one

EBITDA is $152.582m × 0.95 = $144.953m. Capex is $39.104m × 0.95 = $37.149m. Revenue drops by $49.749m, releasing $2.487m of working capital under the 5% rule. Taxes are zero because $144.953m − $57.150m D&A − $91.549m interest is negative. Cash available for debt service is therefore about $110.292m. The interest is $915.492m × 10%, and principal due is 2% × $915.492m = $18.310m. Headroom over the $25m cash floor is $0.433m. A 75% sweep of this excess pays down another $0.324m, leaving $25.108m of cash and $896.858m of debt.
