# Sources and data dictionary

- TaskUs FY2024 Form 10-K, accession 0001829864-25-000016, filed Mar. 6, 2025 and retrieved 2026-11-18. The [input ledger](historical.csv) extracts individual historical values in USD millions by dividing filing amounts in thousands by 1,000. Gross interest-bearing debt at entry is approximated using current plus long-term carrying amounts. Lease liabilities are excluded and operating lease expenses remain in operating costs. Operating income $92.424m plus depreciation $40.223m and amortization $19.935m gives $152.582m operating EBITDA. All other operating expenses, including stock-based compensation, remain in the baseline. No management adjusted EBITDA or acquisition forecast is used. Filings are linked without redistributing source documents. [EDGAR filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1829864/000182986425000016/task-20241231.htm)
- Kaplan and Strömberg, 2008 working paper. The landing page returned HTTP 403 on direct fetch, and NBER’s PDF was accessible. The paper supplies institutional context. [PDF](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w14207/w14207.pdf)

Annual observations in the cash waterfall are hypothetical UTC-independent year-end dates. Value quantities are in millions of US dollars. Rates, growth and IRR are decimal fractions, with annual compounding and actual days/365 for IRR. MOIC divides gross sale receipts by all equity contributed. IRR uses net annual dated amounts and verifies the sign pattern before bracketing a root. Headroom subtracts minimum liquidity and scheduled mandatory amortization before cures. Shortfalls that exceed cure capacity trigger stressed sale. Unpaid interest during forced exit is carried as a debt claim. Net debt/EBITDA above 7× is a monitoring flag.

The sensitivity and joint tables include assumed exit multiple or year, coupon/rate changes, returns, cure-dependent headroom and flags. Reported loan terms, cash taxes, covenants, exit prices and cash flow projections belong to the stylized scenarios.
