# Research protocol

Frozen 2026-11-18 after checking TaskUs FY2024 Form 10-K. This is a hypothetical buyout with entry accounting dated Dec. 31, 2024 and annual year-end periods. Financial inputs would have been available with the March 2025 filing. The historical baseline uses operating income plus book depreciation and amortization, retaining all other reported operating costs. Use entry EV at 10× that EBITDA proxy, new borrowing at 6×, $25m minimum cash, $25m entry fees, a 2% annual scheduled amortization on original debt and a 75% excess-cash sweep. Repay existing debt at carrying amount using acquired cash as a source. Use 25% cash tax on positive EBIT less modeled interest, capex and D&A held at their historical revenue shares, and incremental working capital equal to 5% of annual revenue change. Interest accrues for a year on opening principal.

First compare deterministic five-year base (+6% operating growth, 8% opening rate, 10× exit), downside (−5%, 10%, 8.25×) and severe (−20%, 16%, 6.5×). Revenue, D&A, capex and EBITDA grow at the scenario rate. Cash floor violation is measured after mandatory debt service and before any sponsor cure. Permit up to $60m cumulative sponsor cash cures to maintain the floor. Net debt/EBITDA above 7× is a financing-constraint flag; if pre-cure cash shortfall exceeds remaining cure capacity, invest up to the remaining cap then exit at an assumed 20% multiple haircut. Pre-cure minimum headroom, covenant flags and dollar exit values are measured separately. Exit fee is 1% of modeled EV. Sponsor cash flows include entry equity, end-year cures and modeled sale proceeds. Report MOIC and IRR only when a finite unique sign-pattern root can be established.

The joint stress uses seed 1010 and 800 common Gaussian-copula draws with marginal uniform growth [−20%,12%], rates [6%,16%], multiples [6,12]. Latent growth/rates correlation −0.55 and growth/multiple +0.65 with conditionally independent innovations. Compare fixed rates versus an extra 200 bp after year one and exits in years 3 and 5. Heatmap holds exit multiple at 8.25×, varying growth and rates, and marks negative pre-cure headroom. Attribute exit-to-entry value changes to operating growth at entry multiple, multiple change on starting earnings, their interaction, net debt reduction, fees, sponsor top-ups and any exit equity floor. There is no empirical split. Verify sources and uses, downside year, unlevered and unchanged multiple cases, top-ups and undefined IRRs.
